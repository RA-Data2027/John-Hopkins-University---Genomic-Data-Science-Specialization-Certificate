# Introduction to Genomic Technologies
**Institution:** Johns Hopkins University (via Coursera)  
**Specialization:** Genomic Data Science (Course 1 of 6)  
**Instructors:** Steven Salzberg, PhD and team  

---

## 🧬 Course Overview
This repository / folder tracks my completion of **Introduction to Genomic Technologies**, the foundational first course in the Johns Hopkins Genomic Data Science Specialization. The course bridges molecular biology with modern computational and data science workflows, exploring how biological data is generated, measured, and analyzed at scale.

---

## 📚 Syllabus & Core Modules Covered

### 1. Overview (Molecular Biology Foundations)
* **Topics:** "Just enough molecular biology," the genome, writing DNA sequences, and the Central Dogma (DNA $\rightarrow$ RNA $\rightarrow$ Protein).
* **Concepts:** Transcription, translation, DNA structure, and chemical modifications.

### 2. Measurement Technology
* **Topics:** Experimental tools used to measure genomics data.
* **Concepts:** Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR), Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) technologies, and primary applications of high-throughput sequencing.

### 3. Computing Technology
* **Topics:** Foundations of computer science for genomics.
* **Concepts:** Basic algorithms, memory, data structures, execution efficiency, software engineering principles, and core computational biology software.

### 4. Data Science Technology
* **Topics:** Managing, analyzing, and drawing conclusions from massive sequencing outputs.
* **Concepts:** Reproducibility, exploratory analysis, foundational statistics, question types, the central dogma of inference, experimental design, confounding factors, statistical power, and correlation vs. causation.

## 📝 Final Project Description
The course concludes with a practical literature-based assessment that connects classroom concepts to real-world scientific breakthroughs:
* **Objective:** Review and critically interpret a landmark peer-reviewed genomics publication.
* **Article Investigated:** *"Initial sequencing and analysis of the human genome"* (*Science*, 2001).
* **Focus:** Examining large-scale genome architecture, sequencing assembly processes, and evolutionary events such as horizontal gene transfer through guided multiple-choice analytical questions.
